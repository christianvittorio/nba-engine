# ESPN prop pair orientation
5516 graded lines from 90 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6839; if the SECOND is: -0.7198 (higher is right).
Of the 2596 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 58.9% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
Share of lines where the stat went over: 0.481.

**Verdict: first=over.**
