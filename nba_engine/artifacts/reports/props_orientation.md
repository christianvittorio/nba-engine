# ESPN prop pair orientation
3095 graded lines from 53 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6818; if the SECOND is: -0.7226 (higher is right).
Of the 1487 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 60.1% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
Share of lines where the stat went over: 0.466.

**Verdict: first=over.**
