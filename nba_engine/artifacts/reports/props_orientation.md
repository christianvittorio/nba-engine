# ESPN prop pair orientation
8129 graded lines from 128 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6816; if the SECOND is: -0.7223 (higher is right).
Of the 3858 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 60.1% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
