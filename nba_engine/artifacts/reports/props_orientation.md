# ESPN prop pair orientation
4133 graded lines from 67 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6825; if the SECOND is: -0.7217 (higher is right).
Of the 1964 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 59.9% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
Share of lines where the stat went over: 0.474.

**Verdict: first=over.**
