# ESPN prop pair orientation
1829 graded lines from 32 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6813; if the SECOND is: -0.7233 (higher is right).
Of the 881 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 60.7% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
Share of lines where the stat went over: 0.472.

**Verdict: first=over.**
