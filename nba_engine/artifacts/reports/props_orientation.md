# ESPN prop pair orientation
2200 graded lines from 39 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6818; if the SECOND is: -0.7227 (higher is right).
Of the 1057 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 60.3% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
Share of lines where the stat went over: 0.470.

**Verdict: first=over.**
