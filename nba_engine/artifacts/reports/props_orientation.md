# ESPN prop pair orientation
9589 graded lines from 154 finished games.

Mean log-likelihood if the FIRST price is the over: -0.6820; if the SECOND is: -0.7216 (higher is right).
Of the 4545 pairs where the prices clearly favour one item, the more-favoured item won 59.7% of the time when read as 'first = over'.
